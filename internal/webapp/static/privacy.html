Privacy policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

This policy covers the SyncTune Telegram bot and its music player Mini App. It explains what is processed when you use the service, what other listeners can see, and how to make a privacy request.

Who operates SyncTune

SyncTune is an independent service, not operated or endorsed by Telegram. The operator of the bot instance you use is responsible for its data handling. Self-hosted instances may use different hosting and media providers.

Contact the operator through support. If this opens a group, ask an administrator for a private conversation about your request. Do not publish personal information in the group.

Data we process

  • Telegram identity and access: your user ID, chat or room ID, and profile details Telegram makes available, such as name, username, photo URL and language. The Mini App sends Telegram’s signed initialization data to our server for authentication, including its timestamp and permission flags.
  • Bot interactions: commands, song queries, URLs and any messages or media you send to the bot, including Telegram audio or video submitted for playback. Telegram determines which group messages the bot receives based on its permissions and privacy-mode settings; administrator bots may receive more messages than commands alone.
  • Music and settings: playlists and their names, track titles, artists, URLs, artwork, durations and sources; playback requests, queues, room settings, language preferences, authorized-user IDs and moderation records.
  • Room activity: displayed profile, presence, chat text, message timestamps, voice participation and mute state. When you enable your microphone, live audio is sent through our voice server to the other voice participants.
  • Connection and diagnostics: servers and infrastructure receive your IP address and ordinary request information. Application logs can include user and room IDs, connection events and error details. If a Telegram logging chat is configured, bot-join events can send a group’s chat ID there.

SyncTune does not request your phone number, contacts, precise location, Telegram password, or login codes. Information you voluntarily send in a message can still reach the bot or support operator; avoid including sensitive details.

How we use it

We process this information to authenticate you, apply room permissions, find and deliver tracks, synchronize playback, save playlists, provide room chat and voice, prevent abuse, diagnose failures and respond to support requests.

The current application does not include advertising trackers or sell user data. It does not use room messages or voice audio to train AI models.

Shared rooms and services

Other listeners: your displayed name, available avatar, user ID, presence and permissions can be included in room state. Track requests, room chat and voice participation are shared with listeners in that room. Rooms should not be treated as private conversations: anyone who can access a room may be able to view its activity, including browser listeners. Others can copy messages or record audio independently.

Service providers: Telegram supplies bot messaging, profile data and Mini App integration. Hosting and database providers process service data. Configured search and download services, including the default OneGrab API and supported music platforms, receive queries or track links needed to find or retrieve media. Requests for artwork, avatars, external streams and scripts can go directly from your device to their providers, revealing your IP address and ordinary request metadata.

The player loads Telegram’s Mini App script and the HLS playback library from jsDelivr. Voice connections use Google’s public STUN service for network connectivity; that service receives network information, including IP addresses. Voice audio is relayed by SyncTune’s server, not recorded by SyncTune, and is not end-to-end encrypted between listeners.

Operators may access data for maintenance, support and moderation, or disclose it when legally required. Providers and external links have their own policies; Telegram’s handling is described in its privacy policy.

Storage and retention

Database: user and chat IDs, playlists, room settings, permissions, language preferences and moderation records are stored persistently. These records currently have no automatic expiry and remain until removed. Operators must remove personal data without undue delay on a verified deletion request, when no longer needed for the service, or when operations end, except where retention is legally required.

Room memory: playback state, queues, connected profiles, voice state and up to 50 recent room-chat messages are held in server memory. This chat history is not saved to the application database, but has no timed expiry while retained in memory; it is lost when the server restarts or the relevant cache is cleared. Disconnecting alone does not delete the room’s recent messages.

Your device: the player uses local storage for theme, volume, mute and noise-suppression preferences and recent tracks for each room. This persists until you clear the site’s storage. The player also keeps your room ID and signed Telegram login data in tab session storage to support refresh and reconnect. Open in Browser transfers that login to your browser through a temporary URL fragment, which the player removes on arrival. The application does not set tracking cookies or include analytics scripts.

Other storage: downloaded media can remain in the server’s downloads directory, and the Telegram client library may cache Telegram data on the server. Application and hosting logs, backups and Telegram logging-chat messages follow the operator’s infrastructure retention settings. The current code does not enforce a universal deletion schedule for these stores; contact the operator for the settings of this deployment.

Your choices and requests

  • Microphone: voice chat is optional. Microphone capture requires device permission. You can mute, leave voice chat, or revoke permission in your device or browser settings. SyncTune does not request camera access.
  • Bot messages: the Mini App requests permission separately for the bot to message you. You can decline and continue listening; some playback controls require this permission. Block the bot in Telegram to stop future contact.
  • Playlists and local data: delete a playlist or remove its tracks in the player’s Library or with the bot’s playlist commands. Clear this site’s data in your browser or Telegram webview to remove local preferences and recent tracks.
  • Access, correction or deletion: contact support and request a private conversation with the operator. Identify the bot instance and make the request from the associated Telegram account where possible. The operator may verify account ownership; never provide passwords or login codes.

Blocking the bot, removing it from a group, or closing the Mini App does not automatically erase the database, logs or local storage. Deletion requests are handled by the operator; there is no automatic account-deletion command. Deleting SyncTune’s copy cannot remove copies already kept by other listeners, Telegram or independent providers. Any legally required retention should be explained by the operator when responding.

Security

The server verifies signed Telegram initialization data and checks permissions for authenticated actions. Voice transport uses WebRTC encryption between your device and the voice server. This does not make room chat or voice a Telegram Secret Chat. No online service can guarantee complete security; do not use shared rooms to exchange confidential information.
