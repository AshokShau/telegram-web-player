Meet in your Telegram group.

The bot connects your chat to its listening room.

  1. Add the bot to your group

    Use the bot’s /start menu in Telegram to add it.

  2. Choose the first track

    Send /play with a song name or supported link.

  3. Open the room together

    Send /player, open its button, and choose Listen together. Telegram sign-in is needed to search, save playlists, and send messages.

Know what you share.

Listening rooms are shared spaces. Other listeners can see your displayed profile, track requests, and chat messages, and hear you when your microphone is on.
